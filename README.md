# Courses
In this repository I report the courses I followed during my Bachelor and Master Program, and at the Galilean school  of advanced studies.

In recent years, there are three broad areas of physics that I have taken an interest in exploring further, and which I would like to continue pursuing. Below I report a short list of topics I studied via courses and projects.
- Theoretical Physics of Fundamental Interactions
  Phenomenology and modern theoretical tools for studying the four fundamental interactions of physics.
  	Physics Background: Theoretical Physics of Fundamental Interactions, Quantum Field Theory, General Relativity, Standard Model,  Effective Field Theory; basics of  Quantum Gravity,  String Theory, Cosmology of the Early Universe.
  	Mathematics Background: Real, Complex and Fourier analysis, Path integral methods.
- Geometric, algebraic, and topological methods in the study of Quantum Field Theory
  application and analysis of tools, drawn from various areas of mathematics, that allow for the study of non-perturbative aspects of quantum field theories.
		Physics Background: Analytical Mechanics, Topological States in Condensed Matter, Gauge Theory; Basics of Conformal Field Theory, Supersymmetry, Integrable Bootstrap, Non Invertible Symmetries. 
		Mathematics Background:	Differential Geometry, Algebraic Topology, Lie Groups and Algebra, Representation of Finite and Lie Groups, Rings and Categories of Modules.
  - Theoretical aspects of statistical mechanics
	Aspects of equilibrium and non-equilibrium statistical mechanics, from a field-theoretic perspective.
		Physics Background: Thermodynamics, Statistical Mechanics, Functional Methods in Polymer Physics, Physics of Complex Systems. 
		Mathematics Background: Probability, Dynamical Systems, Ergodic Theory, Stochastic Processes.
